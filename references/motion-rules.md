# Motion Rules

This module defines rules for transitions, page loads, hover states, and interactive animation across layouts generated by AI coding assistants.

---

### Easing & Movement Philosophies
* **Confidence Level**: Critical
* **Purpose**: Enforce calm, purposeful animation trajectories across generated elements.
* **Reasoning**: Default linear or bouncy transitions feel cheap and erratic. Calm, purposeful movement keeps focus on page layout content.
* **Constraint**: Transitions must remain within `200ms`–`400ms` limits, utilizing standard `ease-out` or `ease-in-out` curves. Bouncy, elastic, or overshoot transitions are strictly prohibited.
* **Example**:
  * **Bad**: Applying custom spring animations that overshoot and bounce during modal openings.
  * **Good**: Applying a smooth, calm `300ms` ease-out transition.
* **Exceptions**: Decorative page loaders.

---

### Enforced Animation Targets
* **Confidence Level**: Critical
* **Purpose**: Keep animation speeds consistent across specific visual components.
* **Reasoning**: Inconsistent transitions across different elements break user expectations.
* **Constraint**: Specific movements must be mapped to these exact classes and values:
  - **Fade up**: Applied to *Entrances* (page sections load) locked at `300ms` using `ease-out` curve.
  - **Scale in**: Applied to *Modals & Cards* active states locked at `250ms` using `ease-out` curve.
  - **Pulse**: Applied to *Loading & Focus states* cycling opacity on a `1.2s` `ease-in-out` loop.
* **Example**:
  * **Bad**: Rendering a loading indicator card that spins or flashes rapidly on a `200ms` loop.
  * **Good**: Pulsing loading states gently using `<div className="animate-pulse duration-[1200ms] ease-in-out">`.
* **Exceptions**: None.

---

### Physical Hover feedback
* **Confidence Level**: Recommended
* **Purpose**: Confirm interactive elements visually.
* **Reasoning**: Plain static elements without hover effects feel broken and unresponsive.
* **Constraint**: Interactive cards and buttons must respond with small scale/position movements:
  - Cards: `translateY(-2px)` + increased shadow depth.
  - Buttons: `translateY(-1px)` + deeper hover shadow.
* **Example**:
  * **Bad**: Setting cards to stay static on hover, or changing their background color to bright red.
  * **Good**: Adding hover classes like `hover:-translate-y-0.5 hover:shadow-md transition-all duration-150` to cards.
* **Exceptions**: Mobile viewports (which lack hover states).

---

### Physical Active Press
* **Confidence Level**: Recommended
* **Purpose**: Deliver immediate tactile feedback during click triggers.
* **Reasoning**: Immediate tactile response confirms key clicks before page triggers load.
* **Constraint**: Shrink buttons and clickable surfaces slightly to `0.98` scale on click.
* **Example**:
  * **Bad**: Having no visual change on a button when a user clicks it, causing duplicate clicks.
  * **Good**: Applying `active:scale-98 transition-transform duration-100` classes to buttons.
* **Exceptions**: Text links and navigation dropdown triggers.

---

### Banned Motion
* **Confidence Level**: Critical
* **Purpose**: Block intrusive scroll-jacking and visual bloat.
* **Reasoning**: Scroll hijacking, floating blobs, and excessive staggering degrade device performance and look generic.
* **Constraint**: The following animation patterns are programmatically banned:
  - No scroll-jacking (intercepting browser scroll).
  - No decorative floating background orbs or rotating gradients.
  - No bounce animations on functional elements (buttons, inputs).
  - No staggered load animations taking longer than `600ms`.
* **Example**:
  * **Bad**: Animating cards to slide onto the page one by one over a `2` second period.
  * **Good**: Loading all list elements simultaneously with a simple `300ms` fade-up.
* **Exceptions**: None.
